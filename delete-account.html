Account and data deletion

You can permanently delete your 12Budget account and the data connected to it here, without reinstalling the app.

Delete your account

Sign in with the email address and password you use for 12Budget. Your password is used only to verify your identity and is not stored on this page.

Confirm permanent deletion

All cloud data listed below will be deleted immediately. This cannot be undone.

This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Data that is deleted

When deletion is confirmed, 12Budget permanently deletes the account and all associated cloud data, including:

  • account identifier and email address;
  • accounts, balances, currencies, and savings goals;
  • categories and budgets;
  • income, expense, and transfer records, including dates, amounts, and optional comments;
  • cloud synchronization metadata.

Retention period

12Budget does not retain your account or cloud budgeting data after successful deletion. Deletion is initiated immediately after confirmation. Data stored locally on the device used for deletion is also removed.

Data on other devices

If the same account was used on another device, data stored locally on that device may remain until the app detects that the account has been deleted. Opening the app while connected to the internet signs that device out and clears its local account data.
